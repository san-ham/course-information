Faculty Course Information

Please review the course descriptions and contents before making your selections.

SSC 200 Visual Communication for Urban Planners (0-6-3)

Graphic communication skills for urban planners; Techniques and applications of computer aided design in context of urban planning; seek, produce, manage, exchange graphical information in design process; introduction to Creative Suite applications for graphic communication and map making.

SSC 201 Introduction to Smart & Sustainable Cities (3-0-3)

Planning, definition of smart and sustainable cities; principles of sustainable urban planning and design and processes that develop and shape smart and sustainable cities; economic, social and environmental challenges facing urban areas; role of innovations in ICTs in shaping future cities; sustainability assessment tools and roles in forming smart, healthy, resilient, inclusive cities and quality of life.

SSC 202 Sustainable Urbanization (3-0-3)

Concept/process of urbanization; theories/approaches of urban sustainable development; concept and measurements of sustainable urbanization/development in cities; urban sustainability initiatives in context of urban economies, land-use practices, urban nature and ethical perspectives; programs/policies to enhance sustainable urban development.

SSC 203 Humanizing the Smart City (3-0-3)

Interactions of cyber-physical-human systems; framework for capturing interactions/dynamics in human-machine systems; applications in user case studies; smart cities initiatives and quality of life; technology-oriented initiatives/social innovation strategies focused on human-centered approaches, social exclusion and inequalities, international case studies.

SSC 211 Land Use & Environment (3-0-3)

Smart/sustainable land use; infrastructure systems and municipal/regional models; land use and sustainability paradigms; effects of urbanization on climate change/environment; city policies/action plans such as renewable energy, resilient water, green buildings, energy efficient transport, sustainable infrastructure.

SSC 212 Urban Information Systems (2-2-3)

Principles of UIS/GIS; applications; attribute/spatial data structures; map projections; spatial analysis/cartographic production; location based services/Internet GIS; remote sensing/GPS; GIS planning/implementation; Saudi/international case studies.

SSC 304 City Design I: Spatial Analysis (1-4-3)

Hands-on introduction to spatial analysis for city design; use spatial data to understand spatial patterns and inform design decisions; nature of spatial data; spatial analysis techniques; use of spatial data in city design.

SSC 305 City Design II: Urban Form and Formation (1-4-3)

Hands-on exploration of urban form/formation, role of urban planning in shaping cities; history/types/factors of urban form; planning tools for shaping urban form.

SSC 313 Urban System Modeling and Simulation (2-2-3)

Models/simulation tools for urban problems; land use, public facility siting, population analysis, resource allocation in congested settings, transportation networks; applied urban analysis; survey tools and techniques for primary/secondary data collection, processing, analysis.

SSC314 Urban Indicators Assessment (3-0-3)

Smart urban development indicators analysis; empirical evidence converted into informatics to improve planning/management decision making; Urban Observatories and service agencies; SDG 17 indicator collection/analysis/presentation local/regional/national vs international; linkage with regional plan assessment/national vision; statistical analysis and quantitative methods in city planning.

SSC 321 Circular Economy (3-0-3)

Fundamentals of circular economy and circular business models; theoretical/empirical economic effects of urban environmental policies; costs/benefits of policies for air pollution, water quality, solid waste, climate change; economic valuation of environmental externality and circular urban development economy.

SSC 331 Sustainable Infrastructure Systems (3-0-3)

Fundamentals of sustainable urban infrastructure; urban infrastructure development, energy, transportation, water, social infrastructure; approaches to sustainable infrastructure development; operations of technical infrastructure and policy/planning insights.

SSC 399 Summer Training (0-0-1)

Continuous 8-week industry period in one semester to gain exposure/appreciation of Smart City Planning profession; on-the-job training in related areas; student writes detailed report emphasizing duties and gained skills/abilities.

SSC 406 City Design III: Digital Urban Twin (1-4-3)

Hands-on digital urban twins in city design; 3D city models, real-time traffic simulations, energy use models; improved decision-making/collaboration/visualization; data collection, data quality, technical expertise.

SSC 407 Senior Design Project (0-6-3)

Capstone integrating curriculum in comprehensive original project; approved smart/sustainable city planning topic supervised by instructor to demonstrate knowledge/skills from four years.

SSC422 Management of Smart Cities & Governance (3-0-3)

Smart city management/governance for urban/infrastructure development; urban development/infrastructure management, accessibility/usability; ICT applications; citizen participation/representation; governance processes/tools; legal frameworks, negotiation, managerial/administrative processes.

SSC 425 City Resilience & Climate Change (3-0-3)

Climate change/cities and impacts on urban/natural/built environment; resilience/social-ecological resilience; mitigation/adaptation incl GHG, heat, air pollution; tools, city policies/action plans; green/sustainable infrastructure for adaptation.

SSC 433 Smart Mobility (3-0-3)

Mobility/transportation trends; smart/seamless mobility; transport infrastructure/intramodality; smart mobility as element in sustainable urban transport; stakeholders; MaaS and case studies.

SSC 434 Digital Transformation of Cities (3-0-3)

History/terms/theories/methods of digitization effects on society/cities; technical design, usage patterns, mutual influence of digitization/society/cities; local/international case studies.

SSC 481 Urban Ecology (3-0-3)

Consequences of urbanization on ecological processes; ecological features/impact of population increase and built-up expansion; nutrient cycling, urban greening, ecological landscaping, water features, flora/fauna, sustainable ecosystem planning.

SSC 491 Artificial Intelligence in Smart Cities (3-0-3)

AI in smart cities/urban mobility; AI enabling smart solutions in energy, water, waste, pollution, noise, traffic; technological/social/regulatory challenges; technology/data availability/reliability, lack of AI skills, ethical AI policy.

SSC 441 Plan Evaluation and Appraisal (3-0-3)

Techniques/methods for assessment of plans/programs/public policies; cost effectiveness, goal achievement, cost benefit, cost revenue; pre/post implementation; development impact assessment, cost-benefit, environmental impact, balance sheet.

SSC 423 Urban Policy Development & Enactment (3-0-3)

Design of urban service systems; plans/design; legislative mandates/regulations; formal enactment; analytic/interactional skills for developing/enacting policies specifying urban planning/city service systems.

SSC 419 Urban Code Development (3-0-3)

Urban Planning Code instances/documents/permits/sanctions; planning documents at national/regional/local/master plan to development/subdivision impacting neighborhood/housing envelopes; frontage, street elements, third place, landscaped areas; land use/subdivision actions; right of first refusal/expropriation.

SSC 435 Sustainable Waste Recycling Systems (3-0-3)

Sustainable waste management across product lifecycle to reduce environmental/social/financial impacts; sustainable waste management and circular economy; best practices worldwide.

SSC 426 City Business Development (3-0-3)

Business strategy/current developments/future opportunities; city trends/challenges; collective business responsibility; public/private partners; economically sustainable environment; wealth, clusters, diversification, commercial revenue; world-class best practices.

SSC 471 Urban Livability (3-0-3)

Concept/principles/theory, metrics/rankings of urban livability; well-being parameters and spatial/policy mechanisms in public space/street design; local/international best practice livable cities.
